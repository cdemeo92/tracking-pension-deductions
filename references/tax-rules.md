# Tax rules for supplementary pension contributions (Italy)

Read this file when you need the annual limit, the deduction rules, or the year is not in the table below. Last verified: October 2026.

## Annual deduction limit

| Tax year | Limit (EUR) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| up to 2025 | 5,164.57 | TUIR art. 10 / D.Lgs. 252/2005 |
| 2026 | 5,300.00 | Legge di Bilancio 2026 (L. 199/2025) |

For a year not in the table, search the web for the current limit, tell the user the source, and use that value. Do not extrapolate: the limit changes by law and a wrong figure silently produces a wrong "still missing" number.

## What counts against the limit

- Employee contributions, employer contributions and voluntary payments, summed across all supplementary pension schemes.
- Membership fees charged by the fund count when they appear in the deductible total on the payslip.
- **TFR conferred to the fund does not count** and is outside this tool.

## When a contribution counts

- Cash principle: a payment is deductible in the tax year in which it is actually paid (value date at the fund). A transfer made in the last days of December can be credited in January and fall into the next year, so suggest paying a few days early.
- Payslip contributions (employee and employer) are already subtracted from taxable income by the employer. They must not be deducted again in the tax return (730 / Redditi).
- Voluntary payments made outside the payslip go in the next year's tax return, or are communicated to the employer before 31 December so they are applied in the year-end adjustment.

## Excess over the limit

Contributions above the limit are not deductible. The member should communicate the non-deducted amount to the fund by 31 December of the year after the payment, so that it is not taxed again when benefits are paid.

## Out of scope

- Extra deduction for people whose first job started after 1 January 2007 (recovery of unused limit in later years). Conditions and amounts change, so the tool does not model it. If asked, search the current rule and summarise it.
- Tax saved in euros (depends on the marginal rate), fund returns and costs.
- The tool gives an indication, not tax advice. The final figures are on the fund statement and the Certificazione Unica.
